Privacy

Lux Sound Lab has no backend. Here's exactly what that means.

Local music

Audio files you import are read directly by your browser and stored in this browser's local database (IndexedDB) on this device. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Profile information

Your display name, username and avatar image are stored in this browser's local storage only. There is no account system and no server that receives this information.

Listening history

Recently played tracks are recorded locally so the app can show you your history. You can turn off this visibility or clear it entirely from Settings.

Storage

Everything above is stored using IndexedDB and localStorage in this browser. Clearing your browser's site data for Lux Sound Lab removes all of it.

Analytics

Lux Sound Lab does not run any analytics or tracking of any kind. Nothing about your usage is collected or transmitted.

About & Help

Lux Sound Lab

Version 1.0.0 · Local-only build

About

Lux Sound Lab is a local-first music player: import audio from your device and get playlists, an equalizer, a visual now-playing experience and library organization — all running entirely in your browser, with no account and no backend.

FAQ

Where is my music stored?

In this browser's local database (IndexedDB), on this device only.

Why didn't my song get a title/artist?

Lux Sound Lab reads what it can from the filename. If it can't confidently split "Artist - Title," it uses the filename as the title and labels the artist "Unknown Artist" rather than guessing.

Does the equalizer really do anything?

Yes — it runs through the Web Audio API on the actual audio graph of whatever's playing locally.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet — this is a responsive web app that works in your phone's browser.
